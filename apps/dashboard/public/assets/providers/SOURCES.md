# Provider logos

OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama, Hugging Face,
Cloudflare, LM Studio, ComfyUI and Stability AI SVGs come from
[Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/master/packages/static-svg/icons).
The bundled `LICENSE-lobe-icons.txt` preserves the upstream license.
The Stability AI mark identifies the Stable Diffusion model family used by Forge.

Model family marks in `../models/` are the unmodified static SVGs distributed by
[Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/master/packages/static-svg/icons):
Qwen, DeepSeek, Meta, and Gemma. The upstream project is MIT-licensed; its license
is bundled as `LICENSE-lobe-icons.txt`. The displayed family is inferred from the
model identifier only. These marks identify integrated model families, do not
indicate their current availability, and do not imply endorsement. Unknown families
use an accessible monogram instead of an invented or remote logo.

InvokeAI's symbol comes from the
[official InvokeAI repository](https://github.com/invoke-ai/InvokeAI/blob/main/invokeai/frontend/webv1/public/assets/images/invoke-symbol-char-sml.svg).

Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners. These assets identify
the integrations; they do not imply endorsement. All SVGs are served locally.
